## 1. Role Overview

The **Tutor / Trainer** is the person who directly delivers the learning or training experience to students.

Depending on the organization, this person may be called a:

* Tutor
* Teacher
* Trainer
* Coach
* Instructor
* Mentor
* Faculty member

The Tutor/Trainer primarily interacts with **their assigned courses, batches, and students** rather than managing the entire organization.

Their primary expectation is:

> **"Tutorog should help me teach, manage my students, and understand their progress without creating extra administrative work."**

---

## 2. Primary Goals

The Tutor/Trainer should be able to:

1. See the batches and classes assigned to them.
2. Quickly access their students.
3. Take attendance.
4. Share learning materials.
5. Create and manage assignments.
6. Track student progress.
7. Provide feedback.
8. Communicate with students and parents.
9. Identify students who need additional attention.
10. Spend more time teaching and less time managing administrative tasks.

---

The overall journey can be represented as:

```text id="3g72vx"

        ↓

        ↓

        ↓

        ↓
```

The Tutor/Trainer's experience should be centered around **teaching and student outcomes**.

## 4. Tutor Onboarding

### Expectation

The tutor should not have to independently configure an entire organization.

The Organization Owner or Administrator should typically:

1. Invite the tutor.
2. Tutor accepts the invitation.
3. Tutor creates or connects their account.
4. Tutor sees the organization they belong to.
5. Assigned courses/batches become available.

```text id="b3l0nq"
Organization Owner
        │
        │ Invite
        ▼
Tutor
        │
        │ Accept
        ▼
Tutorog Account
        │
        ▼
Organization
        │
        ▼
Assigned Batches
```

The tutor should immediately understand:

> **"Where am I teaching, which students do I have, and what do I need to do today?"**

---

## 5. See Assigned Batches

### Expectation

The tutor should have a focused view of only the batches they are responsible for.

For each batch, they should be able to see:

* Batch name
* Course
* Schedule
* Location / online status
* Number of students
* Next class
* Attendance status
* Recent activity

Example:

```text id="z2q0tq"
My Batches

Mathematics — Class 10A
Next class: Today, 5:00 PM
Students: 24

Physics — Class 11A
Next class: Today, 6:30 PM
Students: 18

Physics — Class 11B
Next class: Tomorrow, 5:00 PM
Students: 21
```

The tutor should not be overwhelmed by batches belonging to other tutors.

---

## 6. View Students

### Expectation

The tutor should be able to quickly access students belonging to their assigned batches.

A student list could provide:

* Student name
* Profile photo
* Batch
* Attendance
* Recent progress
* Pending assignments
* Recent feedback
* Important alerts

Example:

```text id="9j45pj"
Batch: Mathematics — Class 10A

Student          Attendance    Assignment
──────────────────────────────────────────
Aarav             92%           Complete
Ananya             87%           Pending
Rohan              68%           Pending
Priya              95%           Complete
```

The tutor should be able to open a student's profile for more detailed information.

---

## 7. Student Profile

The tutor's view of a student should be focused on **learning**, not sensitive account information.

The tutor may see:

### Basic Information

* Student name
* Profile picture
* Batch
* Enrollment information

### Learning Information

* Attendance
* Assignments
* Assessments
* Progress
* Feedback
* Learning history

### Communication

* Relevant messages
* Previous feedback
* Parent communication where permitted

The tutor should only see information that is necessary for their role.

---

## 8. Take Attendance

### Expectation

Attendance should be extremely fast.

A typical flow:

```text id="3l6mws"
My Batches
    ↓
Today's Class
    ↓
Student List
    ↓
Mark Attendance
    ↓
Save
```

The tutor should be able to:

* Mark present/absent.
* Modify attendance when authorized.
* Add an attendance note where appropriate.
* See previous attendance.
* Identify students with repeated absences.

The system should avoid making attendance a complicated administrative process.

---

## 9. Share Learning Material

### Expectation

The tutor should be able to share materials directly with the students they teach.

Materials could include:

* Notes
* PDFs
* Worksheets
* Images
* Videos
* Links
* Reference documents
* Study resources

The tutor should be able to specify the audience:

```text id="q6zjbh"
Material
   │
   ├── Entire Batch
   ├── Selected Students
   └── Individual Student
```

Materials should ideally remain organized by course/batch so that students can easily find them later.

---

## 10. Give Assignments

### Expectation

The tutor should be able to create assignments for their students.

An assignment may contain:

* Title
* Description/instructions
* Due date
* Attached material
* Maximum marks, where applicable
* Submission requirements

Example:

```text id="2j2bof"
Assignment
────────────────────────────
Chapter 5 — Algebra

Complete questions 1–20.

Due: 28 August
Marks: 20

Attachment:
Algebra Worksheet.pdf
```

Students should then be able to see the assignment from their own dashboard.

Depending on the MVP scope, submission and grading can be introduced progressively.

---

## 11. Track Student Progress

### Expectation

The tutor should be able to understand how each student is progressing.

Progress may include:

* Course completion
* Topic completion
* Assignment performance
* Assessment results
* Attendance
* Skill development
* Tutor observations

The tutor should be able to answer:

> **"Which students are doing well, which students are falling behind, and why?"**

A simple student progress view might be:

```text id="k1as7m"
Rohan Sharma

Attendance       68%
Assignments     5 / 8
Assessments     72%
Course Progress  60%

⚠ Low attendance
⚠ 2 assignments pending

Tutor Note:
Needs additional practice with algebra.
```

---

## 12. Provide Feedback

### Expectation

Tutors should be able to record meaningful feedback about students.

Feedback can be:

* General
* Assignment-specific
* Assessment-specific
* Topic-specific
* Periodic progress feedback

For example:

> "Good understanding of fractions. Needs more practice with word problems."

Feedback should become part of the student's learning history.

Where appropriate, parents should also be able to see relevant feedback.

---

## 13. Communicate with Students

### Expectation

Tutors need a simple way to communicate with their students.

Typical communication includes:

* Class announcements
* Assignment reminders
* Learning guidance
* Schedule changes
* Individual feedback
* Questions related to coursework

Communication should respect the tutor's relationship with the student and the organization's policies.

For example:

```text id="n2f2ja"
Tutor
  │
  ├── Entire Batch
  ├── Selected Students
  └── Individual Student
```

---

## 14. Communicate with Parents

For organizations working with children, parent communication is particularly important.

The tutor may need to communicate:

* Attendance concerns
* Learning progress
* Assignment issues
* Performance observations
* Important announcements
* Suggestions for improvement

The organization should control what communication channels are available and whether tutors can communicate directly with parents.

The goal should be:

> **Keep parents informed without creating unnecessary communication overhead for the tutor.**

The tutor should be able to complete most daily tasks in a few interactions.

## 17. Tutor Permissions

The tutor should have **limited organization access**.

They should generally be able to:

| Capability                   | Access                  |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| View assigned batches        | Yes                     |
| View assigned students       | Yes                     |
| Take attendance              | Yes                     |
| Share material               | Yes                     |
| Create assignments           | Yes                     |
| Track student progress       | Yes                     |
| Provide feedback             | Yes                     |
| Communicate with students    | Yes                     |
| Communicate with parents     | Organization-controlled |
| Create courses               | Usually No              |
| Create batches               | Usually No              |
| Manage organization settings | No                      |
| Manage fees                  | Usually No              |
| Manage other tutors          | No                      |
| Manage organization staff    | No                      |

These permissions can later become configurable by the Organization Owner.

---

## 18. What the Tutor / Trainer Should NOT Need to Do

The tutor should not be burdened with organization-level administration.

They should not normally need to:

* Manage the organization profile.
* Manage other tutors.
* Manage courses.
* Manage batch schedules.
* Manage organization finances.
* Approve student enrollments.
* Handle platform-level reports.
* Manage platform settings.

Their primary responsibility is:

> **Teach → Observe → Guide → Communicate → Improve student outcomes.**

---

## 19. Success Criteria

The Tutor/Trainer should feel that Tutorog is:

* **Simple** — daily tasks require minimal effort.
* **Focused** — they see only what is relevant to their teaching.
* **Student-centric** — important student information is easy to find.
* **Helpful** — the system highlights students needing attention.
* **Collaborative** — communication with students and parents is easy.
* **Organized** — materials, assignments, attendance, and feedback stay together.
* **Insightful** — progress information helps them teach better.

## Core Principle

> **The Tutor/Trainer should experience Tutorog as a teaching assistant—not as another administrative system they have to manage.**
